# Understandable sound results

Approved in conversation: retain historical, dialectal and obscure matches, and
explain each result programmatically from pronunciation and dictionary evidence.

Show a sound summary above results, a readable name for supported vowel endings,
and IPA with the matching ending highlighted. Supply a familiar homophone only
when its indexed phoneme sequence exactly matches the result. Keep unknown sounds
in IPA rather than guessing a spelling. Expand known usage abbreviations while
retaining unknown labels. Show a useful source definition, skipping heading-only
senses where another definition exists. Do not infer modern frequency or attach
a historical label to all senses just because one sense is obsolete.

Group results by normalized headword, retain matching dictionary entries and
pronunciation variants inside each group, and paginate groups alphabetically
(with sound distance first for approximate modes). Display the total group count.
No contemporary-first ranking or exclusion of obscure entries. Sound evidence
and meaning belong to the specific entry linked by each variant card.

Exact rhymes retain the existing stressed-vowel comparison. Approximate modes
retain their bounded candidate coverage and clearly identify approximate matches.
Validate grouping, paging, label expansion, homophone evidence, and shoe's /uː/
results. Check desktop/mobile layout and source-entry links.
