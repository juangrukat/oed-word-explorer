# Third-party research sources

The decoder algorithm and character tables were consulted from Plan 9 sources.

- `https://9p.io/sources/contrib/tristan/oed/decompress.c`
- `https://9p.io/sources/plan9/sys/src/cmd/dict/oed.c`
- `https://9p.io/sources/plan9/sys/src/cmd/dict/utils.c`

The source browser labels these files as distributed under the Plan 9 MIT
license. The complete upstream license page is preserved in
`research/plan9-license.html`; the consulted source text is retained in `research/`. Character mappings may include historical
approximations. The dictionary content is separate and is not covered by that
software license.
